🛡️ Push Gateway Architecture

Notifications are dispatched through the dual-layer messaging engine:

  • In-App Inbox: Always persisted immediately in the database with audit tracking.
  • Native Push: Sent to Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) / Apple Push (APNs) device tokens registered by mobile clients.
  • Best Effort Delivery: Unreachable devices or expired tokens are logged cleanly without interrupting transactions.
💡 Configuring credentials: Set PUSH_ENABLED=True, PUSH_PROVIDER=expo, and provide EXPO_ACCESS_TOKEN in backend environment variables.
